    Alarm

    Use {name} for your name, {task} for the task and {minutes} for minutes. Example: "{name}, it's time to {task}." becomes "Abubakar, it's time to do the walk." Name tasks as nouns like "walk" or "homework". The message repeats until you dismiss the alarm.
